# Wat is alarm fatigue?

<div class="text-xl mt-6 max-w-2xl mx-auto leading-relaxed opacity-90">
  Te veel valse alarmen → operator-vertrouwen daalt →<br/>
  <span class="font-bold text-red-600">echte verstoringen worden gemist.</span>
</div>

<div v-click class="mt-10 inline-block px-6 py-4 bg-amber-50 border-2 border-amber-200 rounded-lg max-w-xl">
  <div class="text-base font-bold text-amber-900">30 sensoren × ±3σ × 480 metingen / shift</div>
  <div class="text-sm opacity-70 mt-2">≈ 38 valse alarmen per shift</div>
  <div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-3 italic">Wiskundig onvermijdelijk — geen operator-fout.</div>
</div>

<div v-click class="mt-6 text-sm opacity-60 max-w-md mx-auto">
  Bekend uit medische monitoring: 350+ alarmen per verpleging-dienst.
</div>

# Het 6-stappen rationalisatieproces

  <div v-click class="flex items-center gap-4">
    <div class="w-10 h-10 rounded-full bg-blue-500 text-white flex items-center justify-center font-bold flex-shrink-0">1</div>

  </div>

    <div class="w-10 h-10 rounded-full bg-blue-500 text-white flex items-center justify-center font-bold flex-shrink-0">2</div>

  </div>
  <div v-click class="flex items-center gap-4">
    <div class="w-10 h-10 rounded-full bg-blue-500 text-white flex items-center justify-center font-bold flex-shrink-0">3</div>
    <div><span class="font-bold">Validiteit</span> — welk % is een echte verstoring? <span class="opacity-60 text-sm">(operator weet dit)</span></div>
  </div>
  <div v-click class="flex items-center gap-4">
    <div class="w-10 h-10 rounded-full bg-amber-500 text-white flex items-center justify-center font-bold flex-shrink-0">4</div>

  </div>
